Ian Harley Marshall

1933 – 2016 • Renowned Marine Artist & Architect

Scottish-born watercolorist Ian Harley Marshall was an internationally acclaimed authority on late 19th and early 20th-century naval architecture and maritime histories. Melding structural mastery from his architecture background with fine artistry, his works are celebrated for exceptional accuracy, fine command of atmospheric illumination, and expansive ocean scapes.

His curated visual retrospectives have been displayed extensively at institutions including the Royal Society of Marine Artists, Mystic Seaport, and the U.S. Navy Museum. A former president of the American Society of Marine Artists, his historic naval legacy is cataloged permanently across institutional spaces like the Royal Naval Museum (UK), the U.S. Naval Academy, and the National Maritime Museum.
